Tim Kleier and Devin Fitzsimons present to the Fort Collins Internet Professionals on the evolution of AI in coding, exploring the progression from traditional engineering practices through modern AI architecture and the paradigm shifts reshaping how we build software.

The presentation covers several stages in the evolution of AI-assisted development, examining how developers can move beyond "vibe coding" to more structured and effective approaches to building with AI tools.
